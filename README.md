A K-Pop guessing game, using [Daisuki's database](http://kpop.daisuki.com.br/index.html) of K-Pop music videos. Test your reflexes and K-Pop knowledge by guessing the right song name before your friends!

## 🎮 [Play now in your browser at kpop.gg](https://kpop.gg)

No download or install needed: open [kpop.gg](https://kpop.gg), log in with Discord or play as a guest, and share the room link with your friends.

Prefer Discord? [Add the KMQ bot to your server](http://invite.kpop.gg) and play in a voice channel, or launch KMQ as a Discord Activity.

Join our [official KMQ server](https://discord.gg/RCuzwYV) for help and to play with us! We're open to feature requests/bug reports too!

# Gameplay

There are three ways to play KMQ:

-   **In your browser** at [kpop.gg](https://kpop.gg). Create a room, invite friends with the room link, and start guessing. A Discord account is optional.
-   **As a Discord Activity**, launched from a voice channel's Activities menu, so everyone in the call plays together inside Discord.
-   **With the Discord bot**, as described below.

With the bot, gameplay is initiated via `/play`, which invites the bot to a voice channel. The bot will begin to play a random kpop song based on the game options. Users can guess the song by simply typing their guess. [A more detailed gameplay guide as well as the full list of commands can be found here](https://docs.kpop.gg/GAMEPLAY).
